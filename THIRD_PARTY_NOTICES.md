## Cadencr mark

`logos/logo.svg` adapts the Cadencr "Index Dots" mark, following the
connector logo in
[cadencr-plugin-provider-pi](https://github.com/edjubert/cadencr-plugin-provider-pi):
the graphite disc, ink color, and twelve-point ring geometry are reused.
All twelve dots use the Cadencr brand's standard radius of 1.9 on the 48-unit
grid (`STANDARD_CUT.dotR` in `packages/brand/src/tokens.mjs`). The dots are
connected by a continuous wave, with an orange accent in place of emerald.
The Cadencr mark is licensed Apache-2.0.

## Le petit chat — Mistral Vibe, via Miaou

reference pose in
[Miaou's Chaton.swift](https://github.com/edjubert/miaou/blob/main/app/Sources/Miaou/Chaton.swift).
