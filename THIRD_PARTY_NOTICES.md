## Cadencr mark

`icon.svg` adapts the Cadencr "Index Dots" mark, following the connector logo
in [cadencr-plugin-provider-pi](https://github.com/edjubert/cadencr-plugin-provider-pi):
the graphite disc, ink color, and twelve-point ring geometry are reused.
All twelve dots use the Cadencr brand's standard radius of 1.9 on the 48-unit
grid (`STANDARD_CUT.dotR` in `packages/brand/src/tokens.mjs`). The dots are
connected by a continuous wave, with an orange accent in place of emerald.
